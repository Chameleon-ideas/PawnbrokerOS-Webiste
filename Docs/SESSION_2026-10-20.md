## What changed

### `src/components/BookDemo.astro` — rewritten (app: marketing site)

**Design**
- Section now keeps the global `.section` rhythm and sits on a layered violet wash
  (two radial glows + a diagonal gradient) behind a `z-index:-1` `.demo__wash` layer, bordered
  top and bottom. Neighbouring sections (`DataProtection`, `FinalCTA`) are white, so the tint
  separates the booking moment from the page.
- Left column is now a **timeline**: a hairline connector threads between 47px circular icon
  tiles, each step labelled `STEP 01…03` in a violet micro-caps label above the title.
- Assurances changed from a stacked list to **wrapping pill chips** with green tick badges.
- Booking card: 20px radius, gradient accent strip clipped to the top edge, serif card headline
  (matches `--serif` section headings), and detail rows separated by **hairlines instead of a
  nested grey box** — the old "box inside a box" look is gone.
- Card is **sticky** (`top: 104px`) on desktop, static below 980px.
- Details are rendered from a `details[]` array as a `<dl>`; steps/assurances stay data-driven.

**Bugs fixed**
1. **Section had no horizontal gutter.** `.demo` overrode `.section`'s
   `padding: 120px var(--gutter)` with `padding: 100px 0`, so content ran edge-to-edge on every
   viewport under ~1320px. Now verified at 80 / 40 / 20px for 1440 / 1100 / 390.
2. **Detail icons rendered uncentered and grey.** `.demo__detail-item span` (0,1,1) outranked
   `.demo__detail-ico` (0,1,0) and overwrote its `display:grid` and `color:var(--primary)`.
   The icon wrapper and text are now separate branches (`.demo__detail-ico` / `.demo__detail-txt`),
   so no selector collides.
3. **Calendly popup could fire twice.** `setupCalendlyTriggers()` called
   `removeEventListener('click', launchCalendly)` with a *freshly created* function each run, which
   removes nothing; `DOMContentLoaded` + `astro:page-load` therefore stacked handlers. Binding is
   now idempotent via a `data-calendly-bound` flag, and the handler is hoisted out of the setup
   function. Verified: 1 popup after 2 extra `astro:page-load` events.
4. **Timeline connector dangled** past the last step into empty space (drawn on the `<ol>`,
   `top/bottom: 14px`). Now drawn per `.demo__step:not(:last-child)`, stopping at the last icon.
5. **Headline over-wrapped** to three lines with a lonely "shop." — `max-width` was `15ch`;
   now `540px`, matching the lede/steps measure. Two clean lines at 1440 and 1100.
6. **Breakpoints realigned** to the global 1100 / 760 rhythm (was a lone 960), plus a layout
   switch at 980. Grid changed from `align-items: center` (which floated the short card against
   the tall copy column) to `align-items: start`.
7. `overflow: hidden` was deliberately **not** used on `.demo` — it would create a scroll
   container and silently kill the sticky card. The wash is `position:absolute; inset:0`, so it
   cannot overflow anyway.
8. Added `aria-labelledby` on the section, `prefers-reduced-motion` guard on the pulse dot, and
   `overflow-wrap: anywhere` on the sales email so it cannot push the card wide.

**Removed:** the `#open-calendly-btn` id (nothing referenced it; binding is now by
`[data-calendly-trigger]`, which also picks up any future trigger elsewhere on the page).
